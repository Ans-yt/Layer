# Layer in one minute

You can skip the in-app tour at any time. This is the hands-on version.

## 0:00 — Pick a page

Click **Home** or **Library** in the Pages rail. Use `+` to make a page, the page menu to duplicate/rename/delete, and the page setup block in the Inspector to change dimensions, background, notes, and breakpoints.

## 0:10 — Place something

Click **Insert** in the canvas toolbar, then choose a starter section. Or use the left rail for text, shapes, images, icons, and sections. Local image uploads stay in the project.

## 0:20 — Edit it

Click a layer on the canvas or in **Layers**. Change X/Y/W/H, rotation, fill, stroke, corners, typography, responsive mode, notes, and interactions in the Inspector. Double-click text to edit it directly.

## 0:30 — Move with structure

Drag a selected layer. Blue dotted guides appear for edges, centers, grids, parent relationships, baselines, and equal gaps. Hold **Alt** to bypass snapping. Use Shift-click for multi-select, **⌘/Ctrl + D** to duplicate, **⌘/Ctrl + G** to group, arrows to nudge, and Shift + arrows for larger nudges.

Right-click a layer for **Ask Layer**. Right-drag empty canvas space to make a selection rectangle without opening a menu. Ctrl/⌘ + right-click adds or removes a target from the existing selection.

## 0:42 — Test behavior

Open **Interactions** in the Inspector, choose a trigger and action, then click **Preview**. Preview runs navigation, visibility, state, form, animation, tabs, accordion, modal, focus, hover, and scroll behavior as prototype behavior.

Use the viewport picker for Desktop, Tablet, Phone, or Wide. The viewport can be inspected between presets.

## 0:50 — Ask Layer or connect a provider

Open **Layer AI** from the left rail. Choose Selection, Current page, or Entire project. With no provider, Layer proposes local editor commands. Apply/cancel stays explicit and one undoable action. Use **Connections** to configure an AI provider, optional vision route, MCP, skills, and slash commands.

## 0:56 — Review and hand off

Open **Review page** for contrast, overflow, alt-text, target-size, OCR, and snapshot checks. Click **Export** for a complete `.layer.zip` handoff with structured data, a readable brief, assets and licenses, screenshots when attached, and a runnable preview.

The same document is saved locally and recovered after refresh. Project credentials and unrelated chat data never enter the export.
